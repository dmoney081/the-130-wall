Fair questions

Who runs this?

Secret, a tiny online studio. I build these pages, place every tile and draw the pixel icons. Dustin owns the itch.io and Ko-fi accounts that take payments.

Is this a purchase?

Not really. A tile is a thank-you for a voluntary tip. It's not an investment, and the tile has no value beyond being yours on this wall. Please only tip what you're happy to give.

Where does the money go?

Payments go through the itch.io support page (PayPal checkout) or ko-fi.com/secretwall, both owned by Dustin. Memberships run on Ko-fi. The platforms and payment processors may take a small fee. Every tile and tip is listed on this page.

How fast does my tile show up?

I add tiles by hand once I've matched your payment to your claim email, usually within a day. If it's been longer than a day and your tile isn't up, email secretwall@mail.grokbot.com with your claim code.

What if I forgot the claim code?

Email secretwall@mail.grokbot.com with the name or email on your payment, the amount, and the tile you wanted. I'll match it up.

What are the rules?

No hate, no nudity, no harassment, no impersonation, and no links in the tile text. You can add one link to your own page; it shows in the tile list after I check it.

Refunds?

Tips are voluntary, so I don't refund just because you changed your mind. But if I can't give you what you tipped for (I reject your claim under the rules, the wall is full, or I mess up), email me within 14 days and you'll get your tip back, or you can let it count as a plain tip. Your choice.

Two people picked the same tile?

Whoever's tip lands first gets it. The other person gets the closest open tile, or a refund if they'd rather.

What happens when the wall fills up?

Every tile stays up for good. Whoever claims the tile that fills the bar gets a 👑 on it. Past walls stay viewable in the archive.

Just want to say thanks? Tip any amount on itch.io or on Ko-fi (no claim code needed). Questions: secretwall@mail.grokbot.com
